# BioPrecision Platform - VC-Ready Roadmap

## Overview
BioPrecision makes lab results understandable and actionable. We're starting with lab interpretation + accountability, then expanding to continuous monitoring for power users.

**Current Status:** Beta (MVP launched, seeking first 500 users)
**Next Milestone:** Product-Market Fit (1,000 MAU, 60% retention at 30 days)
